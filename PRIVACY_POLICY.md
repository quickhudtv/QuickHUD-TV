# Privacy Policy for QuickHUD TV

**Last updated: August 2026**

This privacy policy governs your use of the software application **QuickHUD TV** (the "Application") for Android TV devices, created by QuickHUD TV.

#### 1. No Data Collection and Transmission
The Application operates **strictly locally** on your Android TV device. 
* **No Internet Access:** The Application does not request, possess, or use the `android.permission.INTERNET` permission. It is physically impossible for the Application to transmit any information from your device.
* **No Personal Data:** We do not collect, store, share, or process any personal data, usage statistics, or device identifiers.

#### 2. Device Permissions Used
To provide its core functions, the Application requires two sensitive Android permissions. These permissions are used exclusively for local features and do not compromise your privacy:
* **Display over other apps (System Alert Window):** Used strictly to render the HUD control menu interface on top of video players and other apps.
* **Accessibility Service:** Used solely to intercept and remap selected remote control button presses (e.g., Netflix, YouTube, or Guide buttons) to trigger the HUD menu. This service does not log text input, passwords, or personal user activity.
* **Query All Packages:** Used locally to scan the list of installed apps so you can assign them to the quick-launch slots.

#### 3. Children's Privacy
Since we do not collect any information, we do not knowingly collect or solicit any data from children under the age of 13.

#### 4. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

#### 5. Contact Us
If you have any questions regarding privacy while using the Application, please contact us via our official GitHub repository: https://github.com/quickhudtv/QuickHUD-TV
